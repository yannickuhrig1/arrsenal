# Code of conduct

## The spirit

This project touches on self-hosting, a field where people arrive with very
different levels. Someone who does not know what a hardlink is belongs here just
as much as someone who writes API connectors.

## What is expected

- Answer the question that was asked, not the one you would have preferred.
- Explain rather than pointing at "it's in the docs".
- Criticise the code, the decision, the assumption. Never the person.
- Accept that you may be wrong, including when you maintain the project.

## What has no place here

Harassment, personal attacks, remarks about identity, origin, gender,
orientation, age, disability or appearance. Publishing private information
without consent. Displayed contempt towards beginners.

## On indexers and content

PlugArr provides, hosts and recommends no indexer, no tracker, no content.
**This is not a place to ask for any, share any or recommend any.** Posts doing
so will be closed, without debate. This is not prudishness: it is what protects
the project and its users. See [DISCLAIMER.en.md](DISCLAIMER.en.md).

## Reporting

Write to **yannick.uhrig@gmail.com**, or open an issue if the subject can be
handled publicly. Reports are treated confidentially.

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove contributions that do not respect this
document, and will explain their decision when possible.
