# Disclaimer

PlugArr is an installation automation tool. It deploys free and open source
software, widely used, and writes its configuration.

**This project provides, hosts, recommends and preconfigures no indexer, no
tracker, no content source.** No indexer list ships with the code. After the
installation, the applications are empty: what you plug into them is entirely
your choice and your responsibility.

These tools have perfectly legitimate uses: organising a personal media library
built from discs you own, managing freely licensed content, distributing files
you hold the rights to.

It is up to you to respect copyright and the legislation applicable in your
jurisdiction. The authors and contributors of this project decline all
responsibility for the use made of it.

## On the indexer entry step

The wizard offers an optional step to enter your own indexers. It changes nothing
about the above:

- the list presented is the one **your Prowlarr** ships, not ours
- no definition is preselected, highlighted or recommended
- no credentials are supplied: you enter your own
- the step needs no button at all

## On third-party services

Some services in the catalogue integrate sources whose legal status is disputed.
PlugArr can deploy them, because deploying free software is neutral, but it
**never pre-enables those sources**. The choice stays explicit and is yours.
